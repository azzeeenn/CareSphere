FAQ

Quick Answers, Sorted

Head to our Programs page and pick a cause you care about — field work, medical camps, logistics, or outreach. Fill out the short application form, and our volunteer coordinator will reach out within 2–3 working days with the next scheduled event near you. No prior experience is required for most roles.
Anyone aged 16 and above can join. Volunteers under 18 need a signed consent form from a parent or guardian. Medical-camp roles that involve direct patient support require a relevant qualification or prior training — everything else is open to first-timers.
Yes. Once you complete at least one full event or 8 hours of service, you can request a digital Certificate of Participation from your volunteer dashboard. Volunteers who cross 40 hours in a year receive a signed Certificate of Impact, which many use for school and college applications. See Recognition for the full list of certificates and badges.
Click “Donate Now” at the top of any page. You can give a one-time amount or set up a monthly contribution via UPI, card, or net banking. Every donation over ₹500 gets an instant receipt by email, and donations are eligible for tax exemption under Section 80G.
Schools can partner with us on tree-planting drives, blood donation camps, and student volunteering programs that count toward community-service credits. Tell us about your institution on the Partner With Us page and we’ll set up a call with your coordinator within 2–3 working days.
Yes. CareSphere is registered to receive CSR funding under the Companies Act, and we work with corporate partners on employee volunteering days, event sponsorships, and program funding. Reach out via the Contact page and mention “CSR Partnership” as the subject.
Funds are split across active programs — tree planting, blood donation drives, medical camps, and community events — based on need at the time. You can see current totals on our Impact Dashboard, and we publish a yearly transparency report with a full breakdown.
Either works. Most volunteers start with a single weekend event and stay on afterward, but there’s no minimum commitment — sign up for whichever events fit your schedule, whenever they fit.
